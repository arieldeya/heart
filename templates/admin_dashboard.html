🛡️ Database Management

Administrator only

Clear selected records from HeartCare when you need to start fresh. Before each operation, the system creates a SQLite backup in instance/backups. Your administrator account and machine-learning model files are not deleted. Destructive actions require an exact confirmation phrase.

Patient accounts: {{ database_counts.patients }} Doctor accounts: {{ database_counts.doctors }} Predictions: {{ database_counts.predictions }} Medical notes: {{ database_counts.notes }} Prescriptions: {{ database_counts.prescriptions }}

Clear Prediction Records

Removes all prediction records, plus medical notes and prescriptions linked to those records. Login accounts remain.

Clear Patient Records

Removes patient login accounts, prediction records, medical notes, and prescriptions. Doctor and administrator accounts remain.

Reset Doctor Accounts

Removes doctor login accounts only. Patient records, notes, prescriptions, and the administrator account remain.

Clear Medical Notes

Removes all medical notes without deleting patient accounts, prediction records, or prescriptions.

Clear Prescriptions

Removes all prescriptions while preserving patient accounts, prediction records, and medical notes.

Full System Reset

Most destructive option. Removes patient and doctor accounts, predictions, notes, and prescriptions. Preserves the current administrator and model files.
